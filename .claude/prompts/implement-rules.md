Work Teams
- Every team has assigned matches in pool play they must work. 
    - The penalty for missing or being late to a work assignment is at the discretion of the tournament directors, and may include a penalty towards their next match.
- Scores are to be recorded live during the work assignment, NOT after the game's conclusion.
    - Score keeping links can be found in your pool schedule located at longvolleyball.com/live. These links are only able to be accessed by the team scheduled to work. 
- All conflicts are to be resolved at the full discretion of the work team. If a resolution is unable to be achieved, find a tournament director. 
- Work teams are in charge of ensuring games run smoothly and in compliance with all rules as defined on this page.
